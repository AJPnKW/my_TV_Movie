Edit TV List (tv_list.txt)

Helper for maintaining your tracked shows. Format: name | tmdb_show_id | season_spec | tvmaze_id(optional)
To use: 1) Load or paste. 2) Edit. 3) Download. 4) Commit tv_list.txt in GitHub. 5) Run “Build TV Data” workflow.
