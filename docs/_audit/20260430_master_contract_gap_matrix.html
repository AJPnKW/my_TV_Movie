Short Take

  • The biggest real doc gap is the missing explicit version-history / source-extraction register in the master contract.
  • The calendar still needs a sharper seven-column / horizontal-scroll statement in the master contract, even though the runtime already behaves that way.
  • Manage Watch State, asset sizing, and the icon-only header/nav are already backed by both archive evidence and extraction notes.
  • Discover is now an active surface in the master contract, but it still needs a concrete non-local feed registry and implementation proof.
